India is a vast country in South Asia and the world's most populous democracy, home to over 1.4 billion people. Its history stretches back thousands of years, from the Indus Valley Civilization to the Maurya and Mughal empires and the struggle for independence that ended British rule in 1947. The country is known for its remarkable diversity of languages, religions, cuisines, and traditions, as well as landscapes that range from the Himalayan peaks in the north to tropical beaches in the south. Today, India is one of the world's fastest-growing major economies, with thriving sectors in information technology, agriculture, manufacturing, and space exploration.
